# Reddle: Letterboxd for books

A social website for logging and reviewing books, like Letterboxd is for films.

## Goal for version 1

A user can sign up with a username, upload a profile picture, search for a book,
log it with a star rating and review, follow other users, and see a feed of what
the people they follow are reading.

## Decisions made

- **Friend system:** FOLLOW, like Letterboxd. No approval needed. The feed is the logs from people you follow.
- **Book library:** do not preload books. Search Open Library live (chosen over Google Books because it needs no key). A book's external ID is its Open Library work id, like `OL45804W`, and book pages are `book/OL45804W`. The first time anyone logs or reviews a book, save a copy (title, author, cover, description, ISBN, external ID) into our own database. Reviews and scores attach to that saved copy.
- **Ratings:** half stars from 0.5 to 5. One log per user per book to start with. A log may have no rating (empty).
- **Username:** chosen at sign up, unique, lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only, 3 to 20 characters. It becomes the profile link (yoursite.com/username).
- **Profile picture:** JPG, PNG or WEBP up to 5 MB. Cropped to a square from the centre and resized to 256 x 256 JPEG in the browser (canvas), then uploaded to the public Supabase Storage bucket `avatars` as `USERID/avatar.jpg` (one picture per user, replaced on each upload). The public link plus `?v=timestamp` is saved in `profiles.avatar_url`, so browsers show the new picture instead of an old cached copy. No picture: grey circle with the first letter of the username (pure CSS).
- **Sign up:** email and password, plus Google sign in.
- **Staying logged in:** logins are remembered on each device (`persistSession`, `autoRefreshToken` in `js/supabase-client.js`; the login lives in the browser's local storage) until the person presses Log out. Log out only logs out that device (`signOut({ scope: "local" })`). Safari may forget it after about 7 days without a visit (its own privacy rule for sites like ours).
- **Usernames at sign up:** the chosen username is stored on the login account (`user_metadata.username`). A database trigger (`supabase/step2-profile-trigger.sql`) creates the profile row the moment the account is created; it skips bad or taken usernames instead of failing the sign up. As a backup, `ensureProfile` in `js/auth.js` creates it on first login. Any logged-in user without a profile (e.g. first Google sign in, or their username got taken meanwhile) is sent to `choose-username.html` by `js/auth.js`.
- **Clean addresses:** profiles are `u/USERNAME`, books are `book/OL45804W`, authors are `author/OL23919A` (`authorLink()`, fallback `author.html?a=`) (from the site's main folder: `https://cflo13.github.io/Reddle/u/maria`). `profileLink()`, `bookLink()` and `authorLink()` (all in auth.js) make them; opened as local files they fall back to `profile.html?u=` / `book.html?id=`. How it works: GitHub Pages has no such files, so it serves `404.html`; a small script at the top of 404.html works out the site's main folder (`/Reddle/` on github.io, `/` on a custom domain) and writes `<base href>` for it; if the address is `u/...` or `book/...`, `js/router.js` fetches `profile.html` / `book.html`, swaps its head and body in, and loads its scripts in order, so the address stays clean. Otherwise it's a real Page not found. Every other page has `<base href="./">`, so relative links keep working after the address changes. Pages read their value with `pageParam("u")` / `pageParam("id")` (query or path). Old links (`profile.html?u=`, `book.html?id=`) still work and switch the address bar to the clean one with `useCleanAddress()` (no reload). Limit: clean addresses are served with a 404 status by GitHub Pages, so some link-preview apps may show no preview for them (previews were generic for books and profiles anyway). Genre pages stay `genre.html?g=`.
- **Profile links (old usernames):** If no current user has that name, the profile page looks it up in `old_usernames` and sends the visitor on to the person's new name.
- **Back to where you were:** clicking any Log in / Sign up link remembers the current page in local storage for 2 hours (`rememberReturnPage`, a click listener in `js/auth.js`). After logging in, `goBackAfterLogin()` goes there (default: home page). New users still see the welcome page first; its last button becomes "Back to where I was". Google sign in and email-confirmation links land on the home page, which forwards to the remembered page. `safeReturnPath()` only allows our own site's pages (never other websites, never login/sign up pages).
- **Resend confirmation email:** `makeResendButton()` (auth.js) appears after sign up when the email needs confirming, and on the login page after "Email not confirmed". Once a minute at most (countdown on the button); Supabase's own rate limit gets a plain message.
- **Installable app (PWA):** `manifest.webmanifest` (name/short name Reddle, standalone, colours #14181c, start and scope `./` so it works at reddle.org and under /Reddle/; icons `images/icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, maskable `icon-maskable-192/512.png`, made from favicon.svg). `sw.js` (registered from `js/auth.js` on https only) keeps our own pages, CSS, JS and pictures: pages internet-first, files saved-copy-first; older `?v=` copies are replaced. It never keeps other websites (Supabase, Open Library, Google, the CDN) or the login/account pages. No internet and no saved copy: `offline.html` (self-contained, no links to other files). The footer shows an Install app button where the browser offers one (Chrome, Edge, Android), and on iPhone/iPad Safari a "tap Share, then Add to Home Screen" hint. iPhone tags (`apple-mobile-web-app-*`, `apple-touch-icon` now a full square) are in every page's head. To throw away every saved copy, change `CACHE` in `sw.js`.
- **Live search suggestions:** `js/search-suggest.js` (on every page, after auth.js) shows a box under the header search bar while typing: Books (5, Open Library), Authors (3, Open Library author search, small photo or initial), Readers (4, our profiles), then "See all results for ...". Starts at 2 characters, waits 250 ms, cancels/ignores older searches, remembers answers per word for the page. Arrow keys, Enter (opens the highlighted one, or the search page), Escape, click outside closes. On phones the box spans the screen (8px margins), rows are 48px tall. The same file has the shared author helpers (`searchAuthors`, `authorPhotoUrl`, `makeAuthorAvatar`, `yearsText`), so its constant is `SUGGEST_OPEN_LIBRARY` (top-level `const` names must not repeat across our scripts).
- **Author pages:** `author.html` + `js/author.js`, all from Open Library, nothing saved: photo (or initial), name, birth/death years, bio (plain text or `{value}`; links, HTML and source notes stripped; 4 lines with Read more; hidden if empty), Wikipedia link (only real wikipedia.org addresses), "Books by this author" (Open Library search by author, most editions first, 24 at a time, duplicate titles removed). Book pages turn "by NAME" into an author link after asking Open Library for the work's author (`linkAuthorName()` in book.js). The full search page has an Authors section above the books.
- **Design (the look): a quiet reading nook.** Warm, calm, bookish, unhurried: paper, leather, lamplight, wood shelves. Lots of breathing room, soft edges, quiet colours, nothing shouting. Clean = few borders, few competing colours, clear spacing. Rules for all future work:
  - **Colours only from the tokens** at the top of `css/style.css`. No hex/rgb anywhere else in style.css or in any JS file (JS reads tokens with `themeColour("--name")` in auth.js). The only literal colours outside the tokens: the tiny theme script in each page's head and `manifest.webmanifest` (they run before the CSS), `offline.html` (self-contained) and the email HTML in the Edge Function.
  - **Night (default), warm lamplight:** bg #1d1714, surface (cards, header) #271f1a, panel (inputs/hover/chips) #322820, text #eadfcc, muted #a8977f, accent soft amber #d9a35f (hover #e8b777, text on it #1d1714), success olive #98a56b, error muted brick #d27a68, border #3b3027.
  - **Day, cream and leather:** bg #f5ecd9, surface #fbf5e6, panel #ece0c8, text #3a2a20, muted #72604b, accent leather #93552a (hover #7f4720, text on it #fbf5e6), success olive #5b6638, error brick #a4473a, border #dccdb0. (Accent, muted and olive were darkened a touch from the owner's starting values so text on panels passes 4.5:1 contrast.)
  - **Accent** is for buttons, links, stars/ratings, progress and chart bars, focus rings. Stars are never bright yellow. **Olive (success)** is rare: success messages and the "want to read" outline. **Brick (error)**: errors, delete buttons, the liked heart, the notification badge.
  - **Fonts:** headings, the logo and big numbers in a bookish serif (`--font-serif`: Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Book Antiqua, Georgia); everything else in the system sans (`--font-sans`). Both are fonts people already have, so nothing downloads and it works offline.
  - **Spacing:** use `--space-1` to `--space-7` (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48px). Pages have generous top/bottom padding (48px).
  - **Shapes:** `--radius-small` 6, `--radius` 10, `--radius-large` 16 (cards), `--radius-pill` (buttons, chips, search box). Cards: surface colour + soft warm shadow (`--card-shadow`), no border. Lines (`--border`) only where they separate things.
  - **Buttons:** filled accent pills for the main action; quiet ones (`.button-secondary`) are transparent with a soft outline and a panel-coloured hover; link-buttons are muted text that turns accent on hover. One focus ring everywhere (accent).
  - **Reading text:** reviews, book descriptions, author bios and diary snippets use the serif at 1.05rem with 1.65 line height (like a page of a book). Interface text stays sans.
  - **Covers** have a book-like shape (spine side tighter) and a soft two-layer shadow; missing covers are panel-coloured with the title in serif. **Chips** (genre tags, pills, choices, people) are panel-coloured pills with no border. **Lists** (log cards, search results, diary) are separated by one soft `--border` line, never boxed.
  - **Logo/icons:** three spines in olive #98a56b, amber #d9a35f and brick #c4705a on a lamplight tile #1d1714 with a cream shelf (`favicon.svg`; the PNG icons and `images/share.png` are made from it with a browser script in Claude's scratch folder).
  - **Day/night switch:** the head script sets `data-theme` before the CSS loads (no flash); pressing the sun/moon fades colours for 0.4s (`.theme-fading`, added by `showTheme(theme, true)`) and the icons cross-fade with a small turn. Reduced-motion users get no animation.
- **Feedback:** logged-in people get Feedback and Invite friends in the footer (`addFooterExtras()` in auth.js; there's no account menu any more). Feedback opens a pop-up (`<dialog>`, `makeDialog()`), max 2000 characters, saved to the `feedback` table with the page it was sent from (`step16-feedback.sql`: anyone logged in can send, only admins can read/delete). The admin page lists it under Feedback with a Delete button.
- **Share and invite:** a Share link on every profile and book page (`makeShareButton()`): the phone's share sheet when there is one, otherwise it copies the clean link and says "Link copied!". Invite friends (footer, and on your own profile) opens a ready-made message with your profile link, editable, with Share / Copy message.
- **Loading placeholders:** grey shimmer boxes (`.skeleton`, `.sk-*` in style.css, `--skeleton` / `--shimmer` tokens) are written straight into the HTML of the home feed, profile and book tops and their log lists, author, popular and genre grids; the home rows use `.tile-placeholder`. `showPagedLogs()` clears them when the first page arrives. Respects reduced motion.
- **Likes:** a heart with a count on every log card (`makeLikeButton()`, `fillLikes()`, `toggleLike()` in books.js). One like per person per log (`likes` primary key), press again to unlike; changes on screen at once and goes back if saving fails. Your own logs show the count but can't be liked. Logged out, the heart goes to Log in and comes back. Counts come from the `log_like_counts` view, only for the logs on screen.
- **Rating breakdown:** under the book page average, a 10-bar chart (0.5 to 5) from the same all-ratings query (`makeRatingChart()` in books.js). Not clickable.
- **Spoilers:** `logs.spoiler`, a tick box under the review. Spoiler reviews show "This review contains spoilers." and a Show spoiler button everywhere log cards appear (`makeReview()` in books.js).
- **Rereads:** `logs.reread_count` + `logs.reread_dates`. On the book page, under your log: a date box, "I read this again" and "Undo last reread" (plain `update` by log id, so the log upsert never touches or resets them). Cards show "↻ Read N times" (N = rereads + 1).
- **Page progress:** `books.page_count` (median `number_of_pages` of the first 20 Open Library editions, saved with new books; older books get it when someone has them Currently reading, via `fill_book_pages()`, which only fills an empty count) and `shelves.current_page`. Book page: Page [ ] of N with a bar while the book is Currently reading; when the total is unknown it asks once and saves it for everyone. Profile Currently reading covers show a bar and %.
- **Reading goal:** `reading_goals` (user, year, target 1 to 1000; `step19`). Profile card "2026 reading goal": bar and "12 of 30 books", counting logs with a date read in the current year. Your own profile: Set a reading goal / Edit / Remove goal. Others see it only when set.
- **Diary:** `diary.html` + `js/diary.js`, clean address `diary/USERNAME` (`diaryLink()`; old style `diary.html?u=`). Logs by date read (newest first, no-date last), grouped by month, day number, cover, title, stars, reread badge, review snippet (160 characters; spoiler reviews say "Review hidden"). 20 at a time with Load more. Linked from the profile.
- **Year in review:** `year.html?u=USERNAME&y=YEAR` + `js/year.js` (`yearLink()`), any year up to the current one, with previous/next links: books read, pages (says how many books had a known count), average rating, busiest month, top 3 genres, books-per-month bars, highest and lowest rated. "Share as picture" draws a 1080 x 1080 card. Linked from the profile and the diary (there's no stats section yet).
- **Picture cards:** `js/share-card.js` (loaded with books.js on every book page): `makeReviewCard()`, `makeYearCard()`, `shareCanvas()` (phone share sheet with the PNG, else a download). Square 1080 x 1080, colours from the CSS tokens (so day or night), covers drawn only if their website allows it (crossOrigin), otherwise a title box. "Share as image" appears on your own log cards that have a review; spoiler reviews say "Contains spoilers" instead of the text.
- **Private profiles:** `profiles.is_private` (Settings → Privacy). Logs, shelves, reading goals and likes of a private person are hidden by the database (restrictive rules using `can_see_activity()`) from everyone except them, approved followers and admins, so they also drop out of the feed, popular rows, book page logs and averages (`books_with_stats` counts only visible logs). Username, picture and bio stay visible. Following a private person sends a `follow_requests` row ("Request to follow" → "Requested"); they Accept (they insert the follow for the requester; the rule checks the request exists) or Decline on `notifications.html` (bell in the header with a count badge). Profile page shows "This account is private" for people not approved. Shared helpers in auth.js: `followState()`, `followPerson()`, `unfollowPerson()`.
- **Block and mute:** Block/Mute buttons on other people's profiles; Settings → "Blocked and muted" lists them (via `my_blocks_and_mutes()`, since blocked profiles are hidden) with Unblock/Unmute. Block (`blockPerson()`): both people's profiles, logs, shelves, goals and likes disappear for each other (database rules via `is_blocked_pair()`, which only answers about pairs that include the person asking), no follows, follow requests or likes between them; the follows and requests in both directions are removed (new rule: you can remove your own followers). Mute: only the muter's list; their reviews are left out of the muter's feed (`withoutMuted()` in home.js); nothing changes for them. Comments don't exist yet: when they're built, add the same block rules to them.
- **Spam limits:** `check_rate_limit()` trigger on logs, likes, follows, follow requests, reports and feedback (`step20`). Per minute / per hour, normal accounts: logs 60/500 (editing an existing log never counts), likes 60/500, follows 30/200, follow requests 20/100, reports 5/20, feedback 3/10. First day after sign up: logs 40/150 (the welcome step logs up to 24 at once), likes 20/100, follows 10/50, requests 5/30, reports 3/10, feedback 3/5. Error text "You're going a bit fast..." (friendlyError passes it through).
- **Email notifications:** new follower, follow request (and comments, once comments exist), each with a switch in Settings → Email notifications (`email_prefs`, all on unless turned off). Database triggers on `follows` / `follow_requests` (`notify_email()`, `step21`) call the Edge Function `supabase/functions/notify-email` through `pg_net`; a failing call never stops the follow. The function (`verify_jwt` off) checks the event really exists (a follow from the last 15 minutes / a pending request), skips blocked pairs and switched-off kinds, sends each event once (`email_log` key) and at most 20 a day per person, then sends via **Resend**. It sends **nothing** until the owner adds the Supabase secrets `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM` (steps in `EMAIL-SETUP.md`). Every email has "Stop emails about ..." and "Unsubscribe from all" links to `unsubscribe.html?token=...&kind=...` on our site (Supabase shows HTML from functions as plain text, so the page lives here), which POSTs to the function; plus a `List-Unsubscribe` one-click header. The function's logic is in `logic.ts` (tested with Node's TypeScript support and stand-ins), `index.ts` connects Supabase and Resend. Redeploy with the Supabase connector's deploy tool (both files) after changing them.
- **Findability:** `robots.txt` (keeps settings, admin, password pages, choose username, welcome, notifications and unsubscribe out; points to the sitemap). `sitemap.xml` is rebuilt daily at 04:17 UTC by `.github/workflows/sitemap.yml` running `tools/make-sitemap.mjs` (Node on GitHub's machines; uses only the public key from config.js): main pages, all genre pages, up to 500 most-logged books as `book.html?id=` (clean `book/` addresses get a 404 status from GitHub Pages, so search engines skip them). Authors aren't listed (we don't store author ids) and profiles aren't on purpose. If Supabase is unreachable it keeps the old book list. The workflow commits to this branch, so **always `git pull` before pushing**. Page scripts set unique titles and descriptions with `setPageInfo()` (auth.js) on book, author, genre, profile, diary and year pages; book pages add schema.org `Book` JSON-LD (`addBookStructuredData()` in book.js: title, author, ISBN, cover, description, pages, average rating). Search Console steps for the owner: `SEARCH-CONSOLE.md`.
- **Header buttons:** when logged in, the header shows separate, always-visible buttons (no dropdown): Profile (your picture + @username), Admin (shield icon, admins only), Settings (gear icon) and a small Log out. Built by `setupHeader()` / `makeNavButton()` in `js/auth.js`. Below 1000px wide only the icons show (so tablets fit). Logged out: Log in and Sign up. The header also has Genres and Popular links (on phones they sit next to the search box).
- **Reports:** a small Report button on other people's reviews (log cards with review text, when logged in) and on other people's profiles. It asks for an optional reason with the browser's own prompt box, then turns into "Reported". `makeReportButton()` and `sendReport()` are in `js/auth.js`; `currentUserId` and `currentIsAdmin` are set there after `authReady`. Clicking elsewhere closes it.
- **Shelves (Want to read / Currently reading):** two toggle buttons under the author on the book page (logged in only). Clicking the active one takes the book off; clicking the other moves it. Putting a book on a shelf saves it to our `books` table first, like logging does. Logging a book takes it off your shelf (you've read it). The profile shows "Currently reading" (only when not empty) and "Want to read" as rows of covers, newest first.
- **Genres:** 30 genres, defined once in `GENRES` in `js/books.js` (slug, name, Open Library subject, keywords, nonfiction flag). `genresFromSubjects()` turns a book's Open Library subjects into up to 4 genres by whole-word keyword matching; nonfiction genres are skipped for fiction (so a WW2 novel is Historical fiction, not History). Genres are saved with the book the first time it's saved. Books saved earlier (or missing a cover/description) get filled in from Open Library when someone logged in opens their page (`fillMissingDetails()` in `js/book.js` + `fill_book_details`). The database only accepts the 30 slugs: if you change `GENRES`, change `step14-genres.sql` too. Book pages show genres as small tags linking to the genre page. The header has a Genres link (on phones it sits next to the search box, and the header shows just your picture, not your name).
- **Logs:** saved with an "upsert" (create or update) on user + book. A log can have just a date. Reviews up to 5000 characters (only checked in the browser). Star picker: click the left half of a star for a half star; arrow keys work too.
- **Feed:** the home page. Logged in: latest logs from people you follow (not your own). Following nobody yet, or logged out: latest logs from everyone, so there are people to discover.
- **Home page rows:** the home page is a stack of rows (built in `js/home.js`), then the feed. Top to bottom: 1) For you (logged in; friends' 4+ ratings, then top-rated Reddle books in your taste genres; a nudge to pick genres if there's nothing to go on), 2) Because you like [Genre] x up to 3 (your top taste genres: favourites count 2, genres of books you rated 4+ count 1), 3) Popular this week (`fetchPopularThisWeek`, topped up with Open Library trending), 4) New and noteworthy (`fetchRecentlyLoggedBooks`), 5) Browse by genre buttons, 6) 3 more genre rows from the most-logged genres on Reddle (`popularGenres()`, falling back to a fixed list) that you don't already have, 7) the feed with Load more. Each genre row = top-rated on Reddle in that genre + popular on Open Library; heading and "See all" link to the genre page.
  - Rules (`fillRow`): up to 12 per row; never a book you've logged or shelved; never the same book twice on the page (rows fill top to bottom, so higher rows get first pick); an empty or failed row hides itself and never shows an error.
  - Speed: only the first 2 rows load straight away; the rest, the extra genre rows and the feed load when you scroll within ~600px of them (`whenNearScreen`, IntersectionObserver). Row results are kept in sessionStorage for 10 minutes (`remembered()`), so coming back to the home page doesn't search again; your logged/shelved books are always fetched fresh.
  - Rows scroll sideways; on computers with a mouse they also get left/right arrow buttons (hidden when the row already fits). On phones the genre buttons are one sideways line.
  - Colours only come from the CSS colour tokens (see Day and night mode).
- **Books you might like (book page):** up to 12 books sharing a genre with this one that average 3.5+ on Reddle (`fetchTopRatedInGenres`), then popular Open Library books from its first genre; never the book itself. Hidden when the book has no genres. `loadSimilarBooks()` in `js/book.js`, loads in the background.
- **Covers:** `makeCover()` in `js/books.js` tries, in order: the stored/Open Library cover; when there's none or it fails to load (Open Library cover links end in `?default=false`, so a missing cover is a 404 instead of a blank picture; pictures under 10px wide also count as missing), `findCoverElsewhere()` asks Google Books, by ISBN first, then by exact title + author. Only if all fail does it show the plain box with the title. When a book is first fetched from Open Library (`fetchBookFromOpenLibrary`), the saved cover is: the work's cover, else the first edition with a cover, else Google Books. Google links are cleaned to https and without the page-curl. Lookups are remembered for the browser tab. Google Books covers are small (about 128px wide), so they can look soft on the big book page cover.
- **Load more:** the feed, profile logs and book page logs show 20 at a time (`PAGE_SIZE` in `js/books.js`) with a "Load more" button while there are more (`showPagedLogs()`; it asks for one extra row to know). Lists are ordered by `created_at` then `id`, so pages never repeat or skip. The book page's average uses a separate query over all ratings.
- **Errors:** every error shown to visitors goes through `friendlyError(error, start)` in `js/auth.js`, which turns common problems into plain English (no internet, wrong password, unconfirmed email, too long, not allowed, expired login). Open Library failures say Open Library couldn't be reached (`fetchOpenLibrary()` in `js/books.js`). If the Supabase library can't load or `db` is missing, `showPageProblem()` puts one red message with a Try again button at the top of the page; it's also a safety net for any unexpected failure (`unhandledrejection`).
- **404:** `404.html` is what GitHub Pages shows for any missing address. Its first script works out the site's main folder and writes the `<base>` tag, so links and styles work at any depth; it also handles clean addresses (see Clean addresses). Opened as a local file its styles won't load, which is fine.
- **Finding people:** the search page also matches usernames (shown above the book results), and every name on a log card links to that profile.
- **Testing:** the owner asked to skip testing between steps until the site is usable, so steps 4 to 6 were built in one go and tested by Claude with stand-ins for Supabase and Open Library.
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages, live at https://reddle.org/ (custom domain bought at Cloudflare; the `CNAME` file in the repo holds `reddle.org`; the old https://cflo13.github.io/Reddle/ forwards to it). The repo is public and Pages serves the `claude/letterboxd-books-ceznit` branch from the root folder. Every push updates the site in about a minute. Supabase Site URL and Redirect URLs are set to this address.
- **Supabase library:** loaded from the jsDelivr CDN with a fixed version (`@supabase/supabase-js@2.45.4`), so there is nothing to install.
- **Supabase key:** only the public "publishable"/"anon" key goes in `js/config.js`. It is safe to commit. Data is protected by Row Level Security rules in `supabase/schema.sql`. The secret/service_role key must never be in this project.

## Stack

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no Node.js or other installs needed
- Supabase for the database, logins and profile picture storage, called straight from the browser
- Free tiers and free hosting to start
- Test on the live site (https://reddle.org/), or by opening the files in the browser

## Database tables

Defined in `supabase/schema.sql` (run once in the Supabase SQL Editor).

- **profiles:** id (same as the login user id), username, bio, avatar_url, is_private (`step20`), onboarded (has seen the welcome page; `step13-onboarding.sql`), favorite_genres (from the taste questionnaire; `step15-favorite-genres.sql`)
- **books:** id, external_id (unique), title, author, cover_url, description, isbn, genres (list of genre slugs; `step14-genres.sql`), page_count (`step18`)
- **books_with_stats** (a view, not a table): each book plus log_count, avg_rating, rating_count and logs_this_week. Use it for anything ranked (genre pages, recommendations, popular).
- **logs:** id, user_id, book_id, rating, review, date_read (unique per user + book), spoiler, reread_count, reread_dates (`step18`)
- **follows:** follower_id, following_id
- **old_usernames:** username (old name), user_id. Filled by a trigger whenever someone changes their username, so old profile links still work. In `supabase/step8-old-usernames.sql`.
- **admins:** user_id. Only the owner (`cflo13`). Users can only see their own row (to show the Admin menu link). In `supabase/step10-reports-admin.sql`, which has a placeholder id; the live project has the owner's real id.
- **likes:** user_id, log_id (one per user + log), created_at. Everyone can read; you can only add or remove your own. **log_like_counts** (a view): log_id, like_count. In `supabase/step17-likes.sql`.
- **reading_goals:** user_id, year, target (one per user + year). Everyone can read; you can only change your own. In `supabase/step19-reading-goals.sql`.
- **follow_requests:** requester_id, target_id (only to private profiles; both people can see and remove it). **blocks:** blocker_id, blocked_id. **mutes:** muter_id, muted_id (only the owner sees their own blocks/mutes). All in `supabase/step20-privacy-safety.sql`, with the helper checks `is_blocked_pair()`, `can_see_activity()`, `can_see_profile()`, `log_owner()`, `my_blocks_and_mutes()` and the spam-limit trigger.
- **email_prefs:** user_id, new_followers, follow_requests, comments (on by default), unsubscribe_token. Own row only. **email_log:** event_key, recipient_id, sent_at (only the Edge Function uses it). In `supabase/step21-email-notifications.sql`.
- **feedback:** user_id, message (1 to 2000), page, created_at. Anyone logged in can send; only admins can read or delete. In `supabase/step16-feedback.sql`.
- **reports:** reporter_id, log_id or reported_user_id (exactly one), reason (max 500), status (`open` / `dismissed`). Anyone logged in can send; only admins can read, dismiss or delete. One report per person per review/profile. Admins may also delete any log and any avatar (`is_admin()` in the rules).
- **shelves:** user_id, book_id, status (`want_to_read` or `currently_reading`), one shelf per user + book, current_page (`step18`). In `supabase/step7-shelves.sql`.

Everyone can read everything (except reports, admins, feedback, other people's blocks/mutes/follow requests, and the activity of private or blocked profiles: see Private profiles). Users can only change their own profile, logs, follows and shelves.

**Limits live in the database too** (schema.sql + step12-limits.sql): usernames, bio 300, review 5000, ratings in half steps, report reason 500, avatars only from our storage, books only with Open Library ids. If you change a limit in the browser code, change the SQL too.
Any signed-in user can save a new book.
