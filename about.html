About Reddle

Reddle is a place to keep track of the books you read, rate and review them, and see what your friends are reading. Think Letterboxd, but for books.

What you can do

  • Search for any book and log it with a star rating, a review and the date you finished it.
  • Keep a Want to read list and show what you're Currently reading.
  • Follow other readers and see their latest books on your home page.

Who runs it

Reddle is a small, independent project. It's free, has no ads, and doesn't sell anyone's data.

Book information and most covers come from Open Library, a free, open catalogue of books run by the Internet Archive. If a book's details look wrong, Open Library is where they come from.
